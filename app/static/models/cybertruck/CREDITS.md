  `public/models/cybertruck-import/`(lossless meshopt compression, axis
  normalization to metres with +Y up / -Z forward, length scaled to 5.683 m).

Runtime adaptation ported to `test-cybertruck.js`: three detached stray
triangle blocks above the cab are dropped, the off-centre body is centred and
widened to the owner-manual 2.03 m, and the single stainless shell is segmented
per-face into glass / cladding / wheels / light bars / cabin roles. Doors, hood
and tailgate are cut along measured shut lines into hinge groups
(`articulate` in test-highland.js). Panel partitions are not a factory rig.
